- An updated comparison matrix pitting Zline against WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram, Discord, and Slack across 20 privacy and performance parameters.

## 7. Anchor Text Profile & Penalty Avoidance

To safeguard against Google Penguin and manual spam penalties, adhere strictly to natural anchor text ratios:

```
[ Natural Anchor Distribution Profile ]
├── 60% Brand Anchors: "Zline", "Zline App", "Zline messenger", "zline.vercel.app"
├── 20% Naked URLs: "https://zline.vercel.app", "https://zline.vercel.app/"
├── 15% Partial & LSI Keywords: "Zline encrypted chat", "real-time calling with Zline", "Zline WebRTC messenger"
└── 5%  Generic Anchors: "visit website", "source", "read more"
```

> **Rules of Engagement:**
> - Never buy links from Private Blog Networks (PBNs) or link farms.
> - Avoid over-optimized exact-match anchors (e.g. avoid repeating "best free chat app").
> - Maintain editorial relevance: links must come from communication, web development, cybersecurity, or software roundups.
